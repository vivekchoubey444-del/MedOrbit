Submit a request

Before publishing this workflow, configure a monitored contact address and a secure process for verifying the requester without collecting excessive identity documents.

What to include

Ask for the type of request and sufficient account/context information. Do not ask visitors to submit passwords, payment-card details or secret credentials.

Response process

Document applicable response timelines, identity checks, exceptions, escalation and appeal procedures after legal review.
